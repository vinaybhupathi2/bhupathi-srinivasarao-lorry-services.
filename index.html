WHO WE ARE

The Leading Husk Logistics Specialists

Bhupathi Srinivasarao Lorry Services has established itself as an industry leader in agricultural husk and bulk cargo transportation.

We primarily serve factories, processing plants, bio-fuel generators, and manufacturing units that require steady, high-volume supplies of agricultural husks (rice husk, groundnut husk, etc.). Operating heavily within Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, we bridge the gap between source mills and industrial complexes.

Vast Logistics Fleet: From 6-wheeler containers to heavy-duty 10 and 12-wheeler open/tipper lorries, we have the ideal transport vehicle for every cargo size.
Guaranteed Timelines: We understand that industrial fuel supply chains cannot wait. We offer rigorous adherence to factory drop schedules.
Interstate Seamless Coverage: Expert routing and documentation across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, meaning zero legal delays at state borders.
